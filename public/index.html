A few good questions

Before you
jump in.

How do I join my first class?

Ask your tutor for the session link or class code. Select “Join a class,” enter your code if needed, and add your name when prompted.

Can I start for free?

Yes. The Free Card lets you join classes and receive practice questions. Submitting practice answers and progress tracking are included with Half and Full Cards.

What is the difference between Half and Full?

Both include practice answer submission and progress tracking. Full Card adds direct tutor questions in class through chat, voice messages, and images.

How do I activate a paid card?

Ask your tutor about card availability, pricing, and activation for your class. This page does not collect payments or activate cards.
